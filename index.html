Help & honest limits

What ParishBook is: the treasurer's record book — contributions by giver and fund, expenses, fund balances, budgets, attendance, a member directory, and clean printed year-end giving statements.

What it is not: ParishBook cannot accept online giving, run cards, or process payments of any kind — it's a single offline file with no accounts and no connection to any payment service. Members give the way they already do (plate, check, your existing online-giving provider); you record it here. It is also not a payroll system, not a full double-entry accounting package, and not tax advice — the statements include the standard IRS acknowledgment wording, but your church's own advisor should confirm anything unusual.

Workflow that works: after each service, enter the count and the gifts (5 minutes with the deposit slip in hand). In January, pick the year in Reports, print all statements, and mail or hand them out — the $250+ gifts are already flagged through the year so nothing is a surprise.

Sample data: the app ships with a fictional church (Grace Community Church) so you can see every screen working. Use “Clear sample data” in Backup & data when you're ready for your own records.
